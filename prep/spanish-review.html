AppealPath: Spanish review sheet

  • Every piece of fixed text in the app, English on the left and Spanish on the right (327 rows; text used in more than one place appears once). The Spanish was written with AI help and proofread once, but no native speaker has checked it yet.
  • Please mark anything that sounds wrong, stiff, too formal, or unclear for someone stressed about a medical bill. Suggest better wording in the Notes column. The app speaks to the reader as usted.
  • [1], [2]… and [service] are blanks the app fills from the letter. Words copied from a letter always stay in English on purpose. 6 rows are the same in both languages (for example, names of English-only forms), shown in gray.
  • Rows go from most to least important: first the results, free help and reason notes that people act on, then the check screen and the first screen, and last the "How it decides" page. Stop wherever you run out of time; even the first few pages help.
  • The "Where" column only helps Noah find the line in the code. You can ignore it.
