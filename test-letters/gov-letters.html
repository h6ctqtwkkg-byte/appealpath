Important Information about Your Rights to External Review

What if I need help understanding this denial? Contact us at (479) 555-0230 if you need assistance understanding this notice or our decision to deny you a service or coverage.

What if I don't agree with this decision? For certain types of claims, you are entitled to request an independent, external review of our decision. Contact the Example Valley Health Plan External Review Unit at (479) 555-0236 with any questions on your rights to external review. If your claim is not eligible for independent external review but you still disagree with the denial, your state insurance regulator may be able to help to resolve the dispute. See the "Other resources" section of this form for help filing a request for external review.

How do I file a request for external review? Complete the bottom of this page, make a copy, and send this document to Example Valley Health Plan, External Review Unit, PO Box 0236, Example City, AR 00000. You must file your request for external review within four months after you receive this notice. See also the "Other resources to help you" section of this form for assistance filing a request for external review.

What if my situation is urgent? If your situation meets the definition of urgent under the law, the external review of your claim will be conducted as expeditiously as possible. Generally, an urgent situation is one in which your health may be in serious jeopardy or, in the opinion of your physician, you may experience pain that cannot be adequately controlled while you wait for a decision on the external review of your claim. If you believe your situation is urgent, you may request an expedited external review by calling (479) 555-0237 or faxing (479) 555-0231.

Who may file a request for external review? You or someone you name to act for you (your authorized representative) may file a request for external review. To name a representative, send us the Authorized Representative form from www.examplevalleyhealth.example.

Can I provide additional information about my claim? Yes, once your external review is initiated, you will receive instructions on how to supply additional information.

Can I request copies of information relevant to my claim? Yes, you may request copies (free of charge) by contacting us at (479) 555-0230.

What happens next? If you request an external review, an independent organization will review our decision and provide you with a written determination. If this organization decides to overturn our decision, we will provide coverage or payment for your health care item or service.

Other resources to help you: For questions about your rights, this notice, or for assistance, you can contact: the Employee Benefits Security Administration at 1-866-444-EBSA (3272) and the Example State Department of Insurance, Consumer Services, at (501) 555-0290. Additionally, a consumer assistance program can help you file your appeal. Contact: the Example State Consumer Health Assistance Program, (501) 555-0295.
